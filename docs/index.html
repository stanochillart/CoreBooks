CoreBooks

Open the book. Let it speak.

Download · Android 15+ · no account · no ads

Night. One small light. A page.

You open a book that lives in the phone. You leave the room. You return. The cursor still sits on the word you abandoned.

When the eyes grow tired, a voice takes that word and walks the line. Not a person in the chair. The book, speaking. You halt it. The sentence waits.

Your files. EPUB, PDF, MOBI. A folder you name. Gutenberg and the other free catalogues when a net is near.

Marks in the margin. A bookmark. No account. No ads. For a reader who sometimes listens, and will not send the file away.
